A final touch on a shot the Body and Aim already built: turn it a little, roll it into a dutch angle, or zoom in. The Body and Aim keep working as they are, so the camera still follows and frames its targets, with the change on top. Animate the settings for effects like a slow dutch in a cutscene.

`zoomScale` scales the field of view from `Lens`. Add it after `GroupFraming` to scale its zoom too.

## Settings

| Setting | Description | Default |
| ------- | ----------- | ------- |
| `tilt` | Turns the camera around its own horizontal axis, in degrees. Positive looks down, like [`PanTilt`](/aim/pan-tilt). | `0` |
| `pan` | Turns the camera around `referenceUp`, in degrees. Positive turns right, like `PanTilt`. | `0` |
| `dutch` | Rolls the camera around its view direction, in degrees. Positive leans it to the right. | `0` |
| `zoomScale` | Multiplies the field of view: below `1` zooms in, above `1` zooms out. Never below `0.01`. | `1` |

recomposer.dutch = 12 * Math.sin(time);
